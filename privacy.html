ExpenseOnTheGo Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

ExpenseOnTheGo is built by Vega LLC. Vega LLC does not collect, sell, or receive copies of the receipts, expenses, reports, or identifiers you store in the app.

What the app stores on your device

The app stores receipt images, vendor names, amounts, dates, categories, accounts, clients, projects, reimbursement states, report settings, reminders, and related expense metadata that you enter or import. That data lives in the app's local store on your device.

On-device AI

Receipt scanning and extraction run on the device. Apple Intelligence is used only when it is available on the device. Optional Pro local models download from Hugging Face to the device and run locally. Receipt images are not sent to Vega LLC.

iCloud Sync

If you are a Pro subscriber and you turn on iCloud Sync, app data syncs through Apple CloudKit to your iCloud account. CloudKit is operated by Apple. Vega LLC cannot read your CloudKit private database. You can turn sync off in the app and manage iCloud in Apple Settings.

Sensitive identifiers

SSN, EIN, and taxpayer ID values are stored in the device Keychain with After First Unlock This Device Only accessibility. The app stores only a masked display value in the local database. Those full values do not sync to iCloud.

Subscriptions

Paid subscriptions are processed by Apple. Vega LLC does not collect payment card numbers. Apple provides Vega LLC with aggregated sales reports, not your receipt contents.

Website

This static website has no account sign-in, no tracking scripts, and no payment collection. If you email support, your email address and message are used only to reply.

Data retention and deletion

Vega LLC retains no app expense data because it never receives it. Deleting the app from your device deletes the local store. Turning off iCloud Sync leaves local data in place. Deleting the iCloud data for ExpenseOnTheGo in Apple Settings removes synced copies from iCloud. To request confirmation that Vega LLC holds no personal data, email info@expenseonthego.com.

Your rights

Because Vega LLC does not collect app expense records, there is no Vega-hosted copy to access, export, or correct. You can export CSV and PDF reports from the app. For questions or a deletion confirmation, email info@expenseonthego.com.

Children

ExpenseOnTheGo is a business expense app and is not intended for children under 13.
