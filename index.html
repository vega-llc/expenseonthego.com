How it works

One guided flow from receipt to close.

1

Capture

Scan a receipt, enter an expense manually, import from Photos or Files, or route a shared receipt into the Import Inbox.

2

Review

On-device AI extracts vendor, date, amount, category, and confidence signals. Missing fields and duplicates are surfaced before approval.

3

Recover

Assign expenses to clients and projects, track reimbursable status, prepare billing packets, and follow up until paid.

4

Close

Generate reports, review month-end readiness, export a close bundle, and lock completed periods against accidental edits.

Capabilities

Built for real expense work, not just receipt storage.

Receipt scanning and import

Capture receipts from camera scans, shared files, PDFs, shortcuts, and manual entry. New items land where they can be reviewed instead of forgotten.

Review Inbox

Approve clean receipts quickly, fix incomplete scans, resolve duplicate warnings, and keep uncertain items out of reports until reviewed.

Clients, projects, and accounts

Organize spending by account, category, client, and project so tax records, client billing, and business views stay aligned.

Reimbursement recovery

Track reimbursables from unsubmitted to submitted, approved, and paid. Export packets and draft follow-ups without rebuilding the trail by hand.

Reports and close bundle

Create PDF reports, supported CSV exports, and a month-end close bundle for tax prep, bookkeeping handoff, or client review.

Reminders, widgets, and audit history

Use workflow reminders, widget snapshots, local product analytics, undo affordances, and audit history to keep the operating loop trustworthy.

Private AI

AI assistance stays on-device by design.

ExpenseOnTheGo is designed around on-device receipt extraction and review assistance. Pro users can also use local model controls, including the default Gemma path or a compatible Hugging Face repository through the app's local model manager.

Receipt images, expense records, and financial details stay on the device by default. When Pro iCloud sync is enabled, data syncs through Apple's CloudKit infrastructure.
