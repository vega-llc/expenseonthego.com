Is ExpenseOnTheGo 2.0 available now?

Version 2.0 is coming soon. The capabilities and plans on this page describe the rebuilt app. Request a launch update to hear when it’s available.

Do I need Pro to scan receipts?

No. Free includes on-device receipt scanning, up to 25 receipts per month, 3 clients, 3 projects, and all 13 PDF report types. Apple Intelligence extraction is included on supported devices. Ask AI chat and the optional Gemma model are Pro features.

Does the AI send my receipts to the cloud?

No. Receipt scanning and AI inference run on your device. Your receipt images, extracted text, and Ask AI questions are not sent to a cloud AI service. If you enable Pro iCloud sync, app data syncs through Apple to your private iCloud database. Exports are shared only when you choose to share them.

What if my device doesn’t support Apple Intelligence?

The 2.0 app requires iOS 26 or iPadOS 26. Apple Vision still reads receipt text, and an on-device rules-based parser handles extraction when Apple Intelligence is unavailable. Pro alone does not make a device compatible with Apple Intelligence or a local AI model.

Do I have to download Gemma?

No. Gemma 3 4B is an optional Pro download of about 2.5 GB for compatible devices. Downloading it connects to Hugging Face, which receives normal request information such as your IP address. Your receipts and AI questions are not part of the download. Afterward, the model runs locally.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes. Subscriptions will be managed through your Apple account. You can cancel renewal in Apple’s subscription settings. There is no separate website checkout. Read the subscription terms for details.
